GROUP BUYING (Kununua kwa Kikundi) – Garantimall

Group buying means many people join together to buy the same product, so the price becomes cheaper because you are buying in bulk.

✅ How it works (Simple):

  1. You create a group order for a product you want.
  2. You share the group link with friends, customers, or WhatsApp groups.
  3. People join and order until the group reaches the supplier target (minimum quantity).
  4. Once the target is reached, Garantimall contacts you to pay.
  5. After payment, we source the product directly from the supplier.
  6. You collect your items at our pickup point.

📍 Pickup Point:

Afya Centre, Travis Building, 3rd Floor, Shop A8

💡 Why group buying is good:

  • You get cheaper prices
  • You buy directly from suppliers
  • You reduce risk of buying alone

We are Garantimall — we connect buyers with suppliers directly and help you source quality products.
