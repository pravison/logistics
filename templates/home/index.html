Our 5 promises

Less hassle. More control over your stock.

01

Buy from any supplier

Buy where you get the best price. You don't need to change your suppliers just to make logistics easier.

02

One collection instead of many

Let suppliers bring your parcels to us, or let our errand team collect them. We coordinate the collections and consolidate everything into one shipment.

Without consolidation 4 suppliers → 4 collections → 4 transport arrangements
With Garantii 4 suppliers → Garantii → 1 shipment

One shipment. One tracking experience.

We notify you when we receive your parcel, finish consolidation, dispatch your shipment, and when it arrives. If there's a delay, we'll let you know.
